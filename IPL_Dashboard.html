Reading the data

  • Mumbai Indians lead all-time wins (92), with Chennai Super Kings and Kolkata Knight Riders close behind.
  • Toss winners take the match only ~51% of the time — close to a coin flip, not a reliable edge.
  • Teams overwhelmingly choose to field first after winning the toss, chasing under lights.
  • Death overs (16–20) generate the highest scoring intensity of any phase, by a wide margin.
  • Virat Kohli and SL Malinga top the all-time run and wicket charts respectively.
  • Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings show the most consistent top-6 form season over season.
